How a claim is decided

Evidence
a page on the provider's own hosts
Validators extract
outage on that UTC date? in scope? minutes
Validators agree if
same yes/no facts, minutes within max(5, 10%)
Credit
min(max credit, minutes × rate) per covered customer
Per day
one approved claim; a rejected one does not lock the day
Coverage
whole UTC days from the day after you subscribe
Claim window
7 days after the day ends
Solvency
the free bond covers one max credit per live customer
Offer a service
  1. 01Promise

    The provider writes its SLA, names the hosts where it publishes incidents and stakes a bond. Customers pay the price; it goes straight to the provider.

  2. 02Claim

    After a UTC day with an outage, any covered customer files the provider's own incident page. Validators extract the facts, not a payout.

  3. 03Credit

    A fixed formula turns minutes into a credit for every customer covered that day, reserved from the bond and withdrawn by each customer.
